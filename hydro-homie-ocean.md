# Hydro Homie gives to the ocean

**10% of what Homie+ earns goes to ocean charities, every month.**

"What Homie+ earns" means the Homie+ subscription and lifetime proceeds Hydro Homie actually receives from Apple for the month: after Apple's commission, taxes and refunds. We donate from our own revenue. You never pay extra, and the app doesn't collect donations.

We pick ocean charities that work on clean seas and coasts. Each month's donation is listed below with its receipt. Naming a charity here records a donation we made; it doesn't mean the charity endorses or partners with Hydro Homie.
